For the check-in, not the final submission

What this is, and what is still left

The aim

A shop owner uploads daily sales. The software forecasts how many units will sell over the next month, and shows whether holidays change that number. The owner uses it to prepare before a busy week instead of guessing.

Ready to show now

  • Sign in and open a demo shop, or upload a CSV or Excel file.
  • A file can be one number for the whole shop: date and units sold.
  • A file can also name each item. The first screen still shows the shop total, then the split.
  • Holidays and the shop’s own sales sit on a calendar.
  • Four models guess the next month: a weekday average, Holt-Winters, ridge regression, and XGBoost.
  • The last month is hidden and scored with MAE, RMSE, and MAPE. The screen uses the model that missed the least.

Still to build

  • Add a deep-learning comparison, such as LSTM, on the same hidden month. It stays off this check-in because a short shop history is easy for it to memorize.
  • Prefer two or three years of history when a shop has it, so each festival has been seen more than once.
  • Turn the forecast into an order: how many of each item to buy, and the last date to place that order.
  • Write the research paper around this test, not around a graph that only looks perfect on the training data.
